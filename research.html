Research

Provably correct, real-time algorithms for autonomous dynamical systems.

Research areas

Trajectory Optimization

We study how to compute optimal trajectories onboard, in real time, for systems with nonconvex dynamics and constraints. Our work on lossless convexification showed that important classes of nonconvex optimal control problems — such as fuel-optimal planetary landing with minimum-thrust and pointing constraints — can be solved exactly via convex relaxations. Successive convexification (SCvx) extends these ideas to general nonlinear dynamics, with recent results guaranteeing continuous-time constraint satisfaction.

First-Order Optimization Algorithms

Flight-grade autonomy needs solvers that are fast, predictable, and verifiable. We design custom first-order methods and conic solvers for embedded platforms, including QOCO, an open-source quadratic-objective conic optimizer developed in the lab.

Robust Control

Real systems face disturbances, model uncertainty, and actuation limits. We develop robust feedback synthesis methods — including robust fuel-optimal trajectory planning with feedback control — that maintain performance and constraint satisfaction under uncertainty.

Optimal Recursive Decision-Making

Many autonomy problems are sequential: decisions now shape what is feasible later. We formulate recursive decision-making problems with temporal and logical specifications, and develop convex formulations that make optimal policies tractable to compute.

Markov Decision Processes

For large-scale stochastic systems — such as swarms of spacecraft or vehicles — we study density control and policy synthesis on Markov decision processes, enabling probabilistic guidance of many agents with safety and convergence guarantees.
