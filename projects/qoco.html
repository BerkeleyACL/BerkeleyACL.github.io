QOCO: Quadratic Objective Conic Optimizer

An open-source conic solver built for real-time, onboard use.

Overview

Trajectory optimization onboard a vehicle needs a solver that is fast, has predictable runtime, and runs on flight-grade processors with limited memory. General-purpose solvers are rarely designed under those constraints.

QOCO is the lab's open-source solver for second-order cone programs with quadratic objectives — the problem class at the heart of most of our trajectory optimization formulations. It is written in C with no external dependencies, making it straightforward to embed, certify, and run in real time.

QOCO is freely available and used in the lab's own flight and simulation pipelines.
