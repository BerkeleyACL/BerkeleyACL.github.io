Pranav Ramasahayam

Master's Student, University of Washington

Profile

Pranav is a master's student studying aerospace controls at the University of Washington, focusing on convex trajectory optimization for entry, descent, and landing problems. He previously interned at Amazon Leo, working in GNC/ADACS. He will be joining the lab at Berkeley in Fall 2027 for his PhD.

Research interests: Convex trajectory optimization, EDL problems, reinforcement learning.
