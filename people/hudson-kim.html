Undergraduate Researcher

Hudson Kim

Undergraduate Researcher

Profile

Hudson is an undergraduate researcher in the Autonomous Controls Lab. He previously worked at Sierra Space on entry, descent, and landing for the Dream Chaser spaceplane, and before that at NASA on wildlife modeling for a satellite constellation and on the build of a three-stage ion thruster.

Research interests: Entry, descent, and landing; propulsion systems.

Outside the lab, Hudson plays badminton and builds rockets.
